## MacSpace

MacSpace 1.0 is the first public release of a free, open source System Data and Apple Intelligence cleaner for your Mac.

- **See what fills System Data.** MacSpace shows the same total as System Settings and breaks it down into items you can understand.
- **Free what is safe to delete.** System caches, old reports and unused system assets go in one step. Version history is deleted only when you ask.
- **Switch Apple Intelligence off.** MacSpace turns it off and deletes the models macOS keeps on disk afterwards.
- **Free purgeable files now.** Other System Files frees the space apps marked purgeable, instead of waiting until the disk is nearly full.
- **Turn off analytics and ads.** Debloat switches off the data collection macOS lets you control, and does it again if macOS turns it back on.
- **Clean automatically.** Choose every day, every 3 days or every week. MacSpace keeps a history of what it freed and can notify you.
- **Stay out of the way.** Use the menu bar, or let MacSpace run in the background with no Dock icon.
- **Look the way you like.** Liquid Glass design with seven themes, each in a night and a day version.
- **Stay up to date.** MacSpace updates itself with Sparkle.

Requires macOS 27 or later on a Mac with Apple Silicon.

Available in English, Portuguese (Brazil), French, Spanish and German.
