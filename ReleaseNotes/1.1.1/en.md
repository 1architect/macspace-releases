## MacSpace 1.1.1

- **Siri, Spatial Photos and Call transcription switches** in Siri & Apple Intelligence. Each turns its feature off and deletes the models it leaves on disk, on every macOS 27 release. Switch it back on and MacSpace restores your settings. Call transcription needs a restart and installs no profile.
- **Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean,** throughout the app and its documentation.
- **About MacSpace,** with the version, GitHub and an uninstaller that restores each module's settings.
- **Unused system assets** also releases unused language data.
- **Scans show their progress** in the action button and on the Home tiles.
- **Quicker, steadier cleanups.** Freeing a row checks only that row, pages stay usable while they refresh, and numbers and words change smoothly.
- **Home page alerts,** a new setting, and short, static (i) descriptions everywhere.
- **Debloat's analytics switches** no longer say Not verified when macOS logged no decision, and **Siri AI search** says what it stops.
- **Many fixes and refinements.**
