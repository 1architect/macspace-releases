## MacSpace 1.0.1

- **The menu bar icon brings MacSpace forward.** A click shows the window in front of other apps, whether it was open or closed.
- **A clearer menu.** Settings… sits on its own, then Open Panel and Quit MacSpace.
- **Updates have their own section in Settings,** with the version of MacSpace next to Check Now. The macOS release is now at the end of the page.
- **Siri's iCloud sync explained.** An (i) on its row says that, while it is on, changes MacSpace makes to Siri also reach your other devices.
- **No more "Not tested" badge** in Debloat.
